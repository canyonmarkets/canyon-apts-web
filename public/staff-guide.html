Canyon Apartments — Staff Guide

Everything you need to manage bookings, inventory, and follow-ups

This guide covers every task you'll do in the Canyon Apartments staff app. Tap any section to expand it. The staff app lives at canyon-apts.com/staff — log in with your magic link email. No password needed.

📋 Daily Routine
Start with the Today tab

When you open the staff app, tap Today in the bottom nav. It shows every call scheduled for today in time order. This is your home base — start here every morning.

Review before each call

Tap a booking card before your call to see the prospect's name, phone number, preferred city, bedroom count, move-in date, and how they heard about us. Use this to prep for the conversation.

Update the card after the call

Tap the booking card and mark it Complete or No Show. Then set the pipeline stage to match where they are:

New → Photos Sent → Application → Approved → Moved In

Running behind?

If you're going to be late for a call, just open the booking card within the hour before the appointment. The system will automatically suppress the "running behind" email it would otherwise send at 10 minutes past the start time. You don't have to do anything else — just opening the card is enough.

Important: Opening cards earlier in the day to review your schedule does NOT suppress the email. Only opening it within 1 hour of the call time counts.
📸 Uploading Photos to a Unit
Step 1 — Open Inventory

Tap Inventory in the bottom nav. You'll see a list of all your apartment units.

Step 2 — Tap the unit

Tap any unit name to open the editor for that unit.

Step 3 — Upload photos

Scroll down to the Photos section. Tap "Upload Photos" and select images from your phone or computer. You can select multiple at once. The photos display on the public inventory page in the order you upload them — the first photo is the main image shown on the card.

Step 4 — Save

Tap Save at the bottom. Photos appear on the public inventory page within about 60 seconds.

Tip: For best results on mobile, use horizontal (landscape) photos. The carousel shows them wider than tall.
🏢 Setting the Apartment Complex Name
What is it for?

The complex name is the real name of the building (e.g. "The Reserve at Desert Sky"). When you send a photo recap email, this name appears in a blue verification box so the prospect can call the complex directly and confirm Canyon Apartments is a current leaseholder.

Privacy: This field is staff-only. It is never shown on the public inventory page — only inside the recap email you send after the call.
Where to add it

Go to Inventory → tap the unit → scroll down to the field labeled "Apartment complex name (staff only)". Type the complex name and tap Save.

What the prospect sees

In the recap email they receive, there's a blue box that says: "This apartment is located at [Complex Name]. Feel free to call the management office directly to verify Canyon Apartments is a current leaseholder." If you leave this field blank, the blue box doesn't appear at all.

📧 Sending a Photo Recap Email
When to send it

After the call goes well and the prospect wants to see photos of a specific unit. This is your main follow-up tool.

How to send it

Open the booking card (from Today or Bookings). Scroll down and tap the "Send Photo Recap" button (purple).

Pick the apartment unit

A list of your available units pops up. Tap the unit you want to show them, then confirm. The system sends them an email instantly with the photos, price, amenities, and the complex name verification block (if you've set it).

What the prospect receives

A branded email with: all unit photos in a gallery, the weekly price, amenity list, city and bedroom count, and the blue complex-name verification block.

After sending

The booking card shows "Recap Sent" with the date. The pipeline stage moves to Photos Sent automatically. AUTO Four hours later, the system sends the prospect an automatic follow-up checking that they received the photos.

⚙️ What Sends Automatically (No Action Needed)
AUTO 2-hour reminder

Sent to the prospect 2 hours before their scheduled call. Reminds them of the time and that you'll be calling them.

AUTO 1-hour reminder

Sent to the prospect 1 hour before the call. Same message, tighter timing.

AUTO Running-behind email

Fires 10 minutes after the call was supposed to start, unless you opened the booking card within the hour before the call (see Daily Routine above). It sends a warm apology and invites them to reply to reschedule.

AUTO 4-hour photo follow-up

Sent automatically 4 hours after you send the photo recap. A short check-in asking if they received the photos and if they have any questions. You don't have to do anything — it fires on its own.

📋 Managing the Waitlist
Who goes on the waitlist?

Prospects who try to book but can't find availability — no units in their city, their bedroom size isn't available, or their move-in date is more than 4 weeks out. They choose to join during the booking flow and land here automatically.

Viewing the waitlist

Tap Waitlist in the bottom nav. Prospects are grouped by reason: City Unavailable, Beds Unavailable, and Date Too Far.

Sending a blast when something opens up

When a unit becomes available that matches waitlisted prospects, tap the amber ⚡ lightning bolt icon in the top-right corner of the Waitlist screen. Pick the unit from the list and tap Send. Everyone on the waitlist who matches that city and bedroom count receives an email about the unit with a "Book a Call" button.

Tip: Before blasting, make sure the unit's photos are uploaded and the status is set to "Available" so the inventory page looks good when they click through.
🗓 Managing Your Call Schedule
Where to set your availability

Tap Schedule (calendar icon) in the bottom nav.

Setting available days and times

Toggle which days of the week you take calls and set the start/end time window for each day. The public booking page only shows time slots that fall within your available windows — prospects can't book outside these hours.

Blocking days off

Toggle a day off entirely if you're unavailable (vacation, appointments, etc.). Changes take effect immediately — slots for that day disappear from the public booking page within about a minute.

Calls are 15 minutes apart

The system books one call at a time, 15 minutes per slot. Once a slot is taken, it disappears from the booking page automatically so you never get double-booked.

✏️ Editing Unit Details
Status options

Available — shows on the public inventory page with a green dot.
Available On [date] — shows with an amber dot and the date (e.g. "Available June 30").
Unavailable — hidden from the public page entirely.

Special badge

An optional highlight chip shown on the unit card (e.g. "$350 off first month" or "Last unit available"). Leave it blank when there's no promotion running.

Apartment complex name

Staff-only field (see the full section above). Never shown publicly — only appears in recap emails you send.

Internal notes

Staff-only notes, never shown publicly. Use for anything you want to remember: maintenance issues, quirks, lease end dates, pending repairs, etc.

Always save

Tap Save at the bottom after any edits. Status and price changes appear on the public page within 60 seconds.

📊 Reading the Stats Page
What the stats show

Tap Stats in the bottom nav. You'll see:

Leads — total prospects who submitted the booking form
Bookings — total calls scheduled
No-show rate — % of completed calls where the prospect didn't answer
Photos sent % — % of bookings where you sent a photo recap (your conversion metric)
Top city / Top beds — what people are asking for most
Lead sources — where people are finding you (Facebook, Craigslist, Google, etc.)
Weekly bookings — bar chart of booking volume over the last 8 weeks

Changing the date range

Tap Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, or All time at the top of the page.

❓ Common Questions
What if someone books but I can't make the call?

Open their booking card and tap No Show after the slot passes. If you need to reschedule, contact them directly — they have a reply-to email address from their confirmation.

Can two people book the same time slot?

No. The system enforces one booking per slot. The moment someone books a slot, it disappears from the public page for everyone else.

Can I send the photo recap more than once?

Yes. The button stays available and you can resend it if you want to highlight a different unit. The booking card will show the most recent recap unit.

What if a prospect tries to book and there are no slots?

They'll see a message that no times are available in the next 2 weeks and to check back soon. Make sure your Schedule tab has availability set so this doesn't happen during active periods.

How do I log out?

Tap Sign out in the top-right corner of the staff app header.

🏠 The Back Office — Tenants, Rent, Documents, Reports (managers)
Who sees the back office

The Tenants, Rent, Documents, and Reports areas appear only when you log in with the management email. The properties login sees just the booking side — so a hired caller can safely be given that login. Extra tabs live under the More button (grid icon) in the bottom bar.

Tenants tab

Everyone currently in a unit, grouped by complex. When a booking becomes a resident, open their booking card and tap Moved In — Create Tenant — their info carries over. The tenant card holds their rate (tap the orange price to edit), deposit tracker, entry/keypad info, documents, and the Activity Log of every payment, email, and event. Automated emails collapse into one "🤖 automated emails" row — tap the + to expand the full list. A payment recorded by mistake can be deleted with the small ✕ on its row.

The money order-of-operations

Record money when it actually arrives. App fee and hold usually come days early: Record Payment → App fee ($25 pre-filled) or Deposit ($200) — the "✓ paid" chips on their card turn green. On move-in day, Record Move-In Charges handles the rest: prorated days (− / + counter, pre-set from the move-in date, dollar amount auto-calculates) and the buffer day (automatic, 1/7 of weekly, rounded up). Already-recorded items show "✓ Already recorded" so nothing double-counts.

Rental agreements & e-signing

On the tenant card, tap 📄 Send Rental Agreement. Nearly everything pre-fills, the move-in money auto-calculates, and Write it for me composes the Special Terms paragraph. Preview the PDF, check which adults get the signing link, Send. They sign on their phone (checkbox + typed name + finger-drawn signature), then upload their photo ID and a selfie so you can match the face to the license. Everything auto-files to their Documents, each signer is emailed their finished copy, you get an alert at properties@, and the PDF ends with a full audit trail (who, when, from where).

No-shows — paid but never moved in

Happens about one in five: they pay the app fee and hold, then ghost. On their card: Mark moved out… → 🚫 Never moved in. One tap archives them, marks the deposit Kept, and keeps the forfeited money on the books as income — it shows on the Reports page in the amber no-shows card.

Phone alerts

One-time setup: open canyon-apts.com/staff in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → open from the icon → Allow notifications. Your phone then pops a banner the instant someone books a call, even with the app closed. To silence booking alerts on your phone only, use the New-booking alerts On/Off pill on the Today screen — everyone controls their own.

Rent tab — Monday mornings

All active tenants, unpaid at the top, with a running collected total. Tap Record as payments land — amount pre-filled, pick Zelle / Venmo / Cash / CashApp. From 10 AM–5 PM Monday the late window banner shows ($50 fee applies); after 5 PM the buffer-night banner lists who is out tomorrow at 10 AM. The send button next to unpaid names pre-loads the right email for the moment. Partial payments are fine — record what they gave you and the row tracks the remainder. Use the arrows to browse any past week.

Late extensions

On the tenant card, tap ⚡ Grant Late Extension — it sends the confirmation email (payment + $50 fee by 5 PM tonight) and automatically logs it. The card badge shows if they have already used their one-time extension, so you always know before saying yes.

Utility overages

When the monthly bill arrives: tenant card → Utility Overages → + Enter bill. The credit is pre-filled and the overage calculates itself. Send the overage email, then Mark Collected when it is paid with their next weekly payment (it shows purple on their Rent row until then).

Emails, templates, and blasts

Send Email on any tenant card uses your saved templates with their numbers filled in — edit anything before sending, and tap Save as template to make your wording permanent. On the Tenants tab, Email All blasts every current tenant (bank holidays, announcements) — and the Make it recurring option schedules automatic sends, like the Sunday-evening rent reminder. All tenant email sends from management@canyon-advisors.com.

Documents & Reports

Documents (under More) is the private vault for rental agreements, photo IDs, and selfies — upload from a tenant card to auto-tag it to them, search by name anytime. Reports shows money collected by type, the weekly chart, occupancy and vacancy, deposits kept vs. returned plus the no-show forfeit card, and the late-payment leaderboard. At the bottom, Export for taxes downloads the full year's payment ledger as a spreadsheet for the accountant.

Move-outs and deposits

When a tenant gives notice, tap Record notice of vacancy on their card — that starts the 72-hour clock (there's a ✕ undo if it was tapped by accident). On move-out day, tap Mark moved out: the unit flips back to Available on the inventory automatically, and the card tells you whether their notice qualified for a deposit return. Then set the deposit to Returned or Kept.
